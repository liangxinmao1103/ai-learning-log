## 二，精选例题
# 七选五专项训练题（高中难度）

阅读下面短文，从短文后的选项中选出可以填入空白处的最佳选项。选项中有两项为多余选项。

Mastering the Seven-Choice-Five Strategy in English Exams

The “Seven-Choice-Five” (also known as “7选5”) section in English exams tests not only your reading comprehension but also your ability to grasp logical relationships within a text. (1) __________ By following a systematic approach, you can significantly improve your accuracy and efficiency.

First, start with a quick scan of the first paragraph and any subheadings. This helps you grasp the main idea of the passage, which is crucial for understanding the context of each blank. (2) __________ For example, if the passage is about study strategies, you can anticipate that the content will revolve around methods to enhance learning.

Next, carefully read all seven options and circle key words such as pronouns (it, they, this), logical connectors (however, therefore, in addition), and nouns related to the topic. (3) __________ These key words act as clues to match the options with the blanks.

When analyzing each blank, pay attention to its position in the paragraph. A blank at the beginning of a paragraph often introduces a new topic or serves as a transition from the previous section. (4) __________ A blank at the end may summarize the paragraph or provide a concluding thought.

Finally, use the “micro-clues” to narrow down your choices. These include lexical repetition (words or phrases that appear multiple times), pronoun reference (identifying what a pronoun refers to), logical relationships (such as cause and effect or contrast), and sentence structure (parallelism or similar grammatical forms). (5) __________ Remember to eliminate options that do not fit, and work from the easiest to the most difficult blank to build confidence.

**Options:**
A. This initial step sets the foundation for the rest of your analysis.
B. However, it can be challenging if you lack a clear strategy.
C. A blank in the middle usually provides additional details or explanations.
D. For instance, if you see the word “strategy” in an option, it may relate to a section discussing methods.
E. Many students find this section intimidating due to its complexity.
F. Moreover, consistent practice with these clues will sharpen your ability to make quick and accurate decisions.
G. It is important to ignore unfamiliar words to save time.
# 参考答案及解析
**答案：1.B  2.A  3.D  4.C  5.F**

## 解题模板对照分析
核心步骤：**抓主旨→圈关键词→析空格位置+微线索（逻辑/词汇复现/指代）**

1.  **第1空**
    - 空格位置：段中（首段观点后）
    - 上下文逻辑：前文指出七选五考查的能力，后文强调“系统方法能提升准确率”，存在**转折逻辑**。
    - 微线索匹配：选项B中 `However` 表转折，`lack a clear strategy` 与后文 `systematic approach` 形成呼应，衔接自然。

2.  **第2空**
    - 空格位置：段中（步骤描述后）
    - 上下文逻辑：前文介绍“快速浏览首段/小标题”的第一步，后文举例说明该步骤的作用，需**指代承接**。
    - 微线索匹配：选项A中 `This initial step` 指代前文的“quick scan”，“sets the foundation” 呼应后文举例的作用，衔接紧密。

3.  **第3空**
    - 空格位置：段中（方法描述后）
    - 上下文逻辑：前文提出“圈划关键词”的方法，后文说明关键词是匹配线索，需**举例论证**。
    - 微线索匹配：选项D中 `For instance` 是举例标志词，以“strategy”为例，说明关键词与主题的关联，符合前文逻辑。

4.  **第4空**
    - 空格位置：段中（并列结构中）
    - 上下文逻辑：前文讲“段首空格的作用”，后文讲“段尾空格的作用”，需**补充段中空格的作用**，形成并列结构。
    - 微线索匹配：选项C中 `in the middle` 对应前文 `at the beginning` 和后文 `at the end`，完美契合并列逻辑。

5.  **第5空**
    - 空格位置：段尾（微线索总结后）
    - 上下文逻辑：前文列举各类微线索的作用，后文强调“从易到难”的策略，需**递进总结**。
    - 微线索匹配：选项F中 `Moreover` 表递进，“consistent practice... sharpen your ability” 是对微线索用法的总结，升华前文内容。
